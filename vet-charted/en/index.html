01A day in the clinic

Six moments from an ordinary day

Follow Max, a three-year-old Golden Retriever, from the moment he walks in until the end of the day.

  1. 08:30

    Reception

    Ahmad arrives with Max. Search either name and the file opens: last visit, weight curve, vaccination status, and a red alert about his chicken allergy before you start.

  2. 09:10

    The examination

    You record temperature, pulse and diagnosis, then write the prescription. What you dispensed moves to the invoice, so nothing is entered twice.

  3. 09:40

    The invoice

    The same items are totalled, you mark the invoice paid, then print it or save a PDF for the owner.

  4. 10:00

    The next vaccine

    Bordetella in three weeks. The dose goes into the calendar and the owner gets a WhatsApp reminder beforehand. If the visit is completed or cancelled, the reminder stops on its own.

  5. 11:30

    The schedule

    The calendar shows what is confirmed and what is pending. Booking requests owners send from their app reach you to accept or to propose another time.

  6. 17:30

    End of day

    The dashboard sums up today’s sales, invoices and the vaccinations that need follow-up, before you lock up.

03Shared record

One animal, several clinics, the owner’s consent

When Max visits another clinic its vet does not start from zero, but nothing is shared without the owner’s permission.

  1. The clinic scans the animal’s QR code
  2. The owner gets a request in the appThey approve or decline.
  3. Only clinical facts appearDiagnoses, vaccinations and allergies. Free-text notes are never shared.
  4. The owner can withdraw at any timeThe clinic loses access immediately.

05FAQ

Questions we hear often

Which devices does it run on?
Windows, macOS, Android and iOS, with a single account.
Does it support Arabic and English?
Yes. The whole interface is in Arabic and English (right-to-left and left-to-right), with a light and a dark appearance.
How do reminders reach the owner?
Through WhatsApp from the clinic’s own number, linked by scanning a QR code, and through notifications in the owner app.
Can several staff work together?
Yes. Each member has an account with permissions set by the clinic owner, and actions are written to an activity log.
Is my clinic’s data visible to other clinics?
No. Each clinic is isolated from the others, and an animal’s record is shared with another clinic only with its owner’s consent.
What happens if an owner deletes their account?
The login is deleted and its link to the animals ends, while the clinic’s records stay. Details are on the account deletion page.
