Delete your account

Delete your CheckBook account inside the app, or send us a request if you cannot open it. Here is what is deleted, what stays and how long it takes.

Last updated: 2026-10-12App: CheckBook
Contents
  1. In the app
  2. What is deleted
  3. Send a request
  4. After your request
  5. Questions

1Fastest way: inside the app

If you can open the app, you can delete your account yourself in under a minute:

  1. Open SettingsTap the settings icon on the home screen.
  2. Tap “Delete account”Then confirm in the dialog.
  3. Sign in again if askedTo protect your account, the app may ask you to sign in again with Google or Apple before deleting.

When deletion succeeds your account and your cheques are deleted and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

2What is deleted and what stays

Deleted

  • All your cheques and their details
  • Your profile: name, email, photo and account ID
  • The notification token and the reminders on your device
  • The sign-in account itself

May stay

  • Backups and technical logs at our cloud providers for a short time until their normal deletion cycle ends
  • Advertising data collected by Google AdMob, which falls under Google’s policies
Cheques and the profile are deleted automatically on the server as soon as your account is deleted, and we do not keep a record of your cheques.

3Can’t sign in? Send a request

If you cannot open the app, send us a request. Fill in the fields and your email app opens a ready message that reaches us at info@inturp.com.

4What happens after your request

  1. We verify it is youWe may ask you to confirm the email linked to the account so nobody can delete someone else’s account.
  2. We carry out the deletionWe delete the account and everything listed above.
  3. We confirmWe reply within 30 days at most, usually sooner.

5Questions?

Write to info@inturp.com. For details on our data practices see the privacy policy.
