Inturp website privacy

Scope

This page describes the Inturp informational website. For Vet Charted application data, refer to the product privacy policy linked below.

Contacting us

Email links open your email app. The Vet Charted demo form prepares an email that you send yourself; the website does not automatically submit it. If you send an email, we receive the information you include so we can respond.

Hosting and fonts

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading a page may provide the hosting service with technical information such as your IP address and request details. We use Google Fonts; loading those fonts sends a request to Google servers.

Preferences and measurement

The website may store your theme or language preference in your browser. You can clear this data in your browser settings. We use Google Analytics to measure page visits, contact clicks and technical information about the browser, device and approximate region, without message text. Analytics load only after you choose to allow measurement. You can change your choice using the privacy settings link.

Questions and requests

For website privacy questions or requests concerning an email you have sent us, contact info@inturp.com.
