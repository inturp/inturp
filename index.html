About Inturp

We build the infrastructure behind new businesses.

Inturp was created to do more than launch ideas. We look for meaningful business opportunities, design the software that makes them possible, and support the broader ecosystem required to turn those ideas into lasting products.

Our approach is deliberate: one opportunity at a time, built with care, and scaled with intention. We believe the best products are born from deep understanding, clean execution, and a clear path to real impact.

What we’re building

VetCharted

Veterinary clinic management, all in one place.

VetCharted gives clinics a reliable place to run their day to day operations: appointments, medical records, patient history, and everything in between.

Manage animal records, examinations, vaccinations, invoices and inventory in Arabic and English.

Al Ameer Hydraulics

Heavy-equipment parts and machines, in one app.

We designed and built the Al Ameer Hydraulics app for a business with seven branches across Palestine, Jordan and Egypt.

Customers browse machines and spare parts, order for delivery or branch pickup, and reach the team on WhatsApp.

Our work

CheckBook

Every cheque, every due date, under control.

CheckBook is a mobile app for keeping track of cheques: amounts, banks, due dates and states, with a dashboard of totals.

Plan a whole series of cheques in a minute with the smart plan, and get reminded before each one falls due.
