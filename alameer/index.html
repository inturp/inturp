The project

A specialist store deserves an app to match.

The business

Al Ameer supplies individuals, workshops and companies with spare parts and tools for excavators and heavy equipment, plus hydraulics. It also sells machines and services machinery and hydraulic hammers at its branches.

What we built

An Arabic-first, right-to-left app in the company’s black and gold, bringing machines, the parts catalog, offers, cart, orders and support into one place.

Behind the interface sit the admin tools the team uses to run products, orders and customers from the same app.

Questions

Questions about the Al Ameer app.

What is the Al Ameer Hydraulics app?+

The app of Al Ameer, a business selling spare parts, hydraulics and machines for heavy equipment, with seven branches in Palestine, Jordan and Egypt. Browse machines and parts, order, and contact the team.

Can I order for delivery or pick up from a branch?+

Yes. At checkout you choose delivery to a saved address or pickup from a branch that supports it: Nablus (Al-Hisba Street), Ramallah (Beitunia) or Jammain.

How do I pay?+

You pay on delivery or at the branch. The app does not ask for card details and does not take online payments.

Can I contact the team on WhatsApp?+

Yes. Every machine page has a direct WhatsApp button, and the app has a support chat and a contact page.

How do I delete my account?+

From Settings, then Profile, then “Delete account permanently”. The details are on the account deletion page.
